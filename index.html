What the contract allows

Holders can transfer tokens and use approve to set a spending allowance. An approved address can call transferFrom within that allowance.

The contract owner can mint additional tokens. The initial supply can therefore increase. A wallet balance or blockchain transfer does not establish liquidity, backing, or market value.
